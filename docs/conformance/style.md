# Conformance — visual style (STYLEGUIDE.md)

Independent check of the restyle against `STYLEGUIDE.md` (source of truth) and the binding decisions of the
restyle plan: full dark mode (Automatic / Light / Dark); where the guide conflicts with the SPEC §13 colour
words, the guide wins. That means today = red, forecast orange, annotation cards neutral with a blue/red 3 px
edge, no amber, burnup markers in the type colour, tones mapped to on-track / at-risk / late, and ideal = gray
`--undated`.

Evidence comes from three sources:

- **Code**: `frontend/src/index.css`, `App.css`, `index.html`, `components/*`, `theme/appearance.ts`,
  `screens/*` and `public/favicon.svg`.
- **Computed styles**: a scratch Playwright probe run in light and dark at 1440 px and 700 px, with and
  without `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Screenshots**: the builder's E-T3 tour (`light-0n-*` and `dark-0n-*`, regenerated by the checker's
  `npm run e2e`) and the checker's own `x-*` shots (tooltip, focus ring, gutter zoom, skeleton, 700 px).

Verdicts: **MATCH**, **MISMATCH** (with severity H / M / L), **MISSING**, and **N/A** (the guide part does
not apply to Heimdall). Line numbers refer to the working tree at the time of the check.

## Visual findings

Overall: both screens read as a native macOS app in both appearances. The hierarchy is clear (17 / 600
title, secondary path, uppercase section headers, 12 px cards on the grouped background), the colour is sober
(hue only on status), and alignment is clean. There is no clipping or horizontal scroll at 1440, 1280, 1024
or 700 px (`06-review-1024`, `x-*-700`, E-V2 / E-V3). The defects found:

| # | Severity | Screenshot(s) | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **M** | `light-02`, `light-03`, `06-review-1024`, `x-light-gutter-zoom` | **Orange on light orange / green on light green.** In light mode the gutter pills "Deviation +24 %" (`#ff9500` on `orange 12% + white`) and "Deviation +10 %" (`#34c759` on its tint) measure about **2.0:1**. That is illegible-adjacent at 11 px, far under 4.5:1. Dark is fine (6.6:1). |
| V2 | **M** | `04b-callouts-crowded` ("Deviation: 12.0%"), any good-tone score figure | Tone figures in light mode: orange and green 13 px / 600 text on white measure **2.2:1**, and red figures 3.1–3.5:1 (on `--fill`). Dark is fine (≥ 3.7:1 red, ≥ 8:1 orange / green). |
